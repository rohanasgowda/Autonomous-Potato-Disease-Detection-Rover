# Future Scope (Suggested Enhancements)

These items describe future enhancements and do not imply missing current requirements. They are candidates for later phases or follow-up projects.

- Physical rover motor integration and low-level motion control drivers.
- Advanced segmentation models (U-Net/Mobile U-Net / DeepLab) for improved severity estimation.
- Expand crop/disease coverage beyond the initial potato classes.
- Multi-rover coordination and cloud synchronization for fleet deployments.
- GPS/visual navigation for autonomous routing.
- Farmer-facing mobile/web app for reports and history.
- Agronomist-reviewed treatment database and rule-based fallbacks for the LLM.
